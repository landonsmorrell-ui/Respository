Escape the Abyss

A giant kraken hunts your tall ship from beneath the waves. Outrun its tentacles, navigate the open sea, and survive as long as you can. Your survival time and personal best are tracked, and your high score is saved in this browser. On phones, hold SAIL to move, steer with ◀ ▶, and hold BOOST for extra speed. On desktop use W/S, A/D, and Shift. Your ship is faster than the Kraken, even without boosting. Forty percent of swells break into whitewater at their crests. Raise the wave-height slider as high as 20 meters for towering seas, or choose Tsunami for enormous parallel wave rows, with 20% forming full wave barrels with an automatic visibility camera beneath a grey sky.
